## What each clip says

- **01** (Redbelly technology): Accountable consensus: anyone who tries to double spend can be identified, because the protocol produces an undeniable proof of fraud.
- **02** (Redbelly technology): Every Redbelly node accepts from day one that governance can upgrade the protocol, so a new version rolls out automatically instead of splitting the chain in two.
- **03** (Project Acacia): In Project Acacia, investors used the AUDM stablecoin to buy into and cash out of a fund through Tokeniser, with each swap settling straight away instead of over days.
- **04** (Project Acacia): Tokenising invoices in Project Acacia showed that the value is in the workflow around the token, where the invoice works as a promise of payment, not in the digital copy itself.
- **05** (Tokeniser): Tokeniser answers the 'how' of real-world asset tokenisation with a familiar web platform that brings issuers, their investors and their tokens onto the Redbelly Network.
- **06** (Tokeniser): Every regulated asset needs a registry of who owns what, so Redbelly acquired Liquidise's registry business and rebranded it Tokeniser to cover an asset's whole life cycle.
